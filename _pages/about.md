Hi! I'm Viraj, a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), with a minor in Management, advised by [Prof. Brian C. Williams](https://www.csail.mit.edu/person/brian-williams) in [CSAIL's Model-Based Embedded and Robotics Systems Group (MERS)](https://www.csail.mit.edu/research/model-based-embedded-and-robotics-systems-group). I am also part of the [Embodied Intelligence community](https://ei.csail.mit.edu/), and I plan to defend my thesis, [*Scaling Risk-Aware Multi-Robot Planning in the Wild through Decomposition*](/theses/), in spring 2027.

I study how **robot teams can coordinate in the real world**, where one robot's decisions shape what is feasible, optimal, or safe for others. Instead of planning jointly for the whole team or learning coordination end to end, I combine reusable robot capabilities, learned or model-based, with explicit reasoning about how robots interact.

My work extends MAPF beyond its idealized assumptions to interdependent tasks, uncertain and changing environments, and shared risk, **coordinating thousands of mobile robots and robot arms**. Increasingly, I am interested in when robots need to reason together, and in how planning and learning can help coordination generalize across team sizes, tasks, and types of robots.

## Research Focus
- **Multi-robot coordination in the real world.** Coordinating teams through interactions beyond collision avoidance, such as shared beliefs about [partially observed](/publication/2026-09-01-belief-aware-multi-agent-path-finding-under-map-uncertainty-13) and [changing](/publication/2026-10-01-belief-aware-lifelong-multi-agent-path-finding-under-dynamic-traversability-14) environments, [information gathering during exploration](/publication/2024-09-19-multi-agent-vulcan-an-information-driven-multi-agent-path-finding-approach), and [task assignments](/publication/2026-03-01-large-neighborhood-search-for-multi-agent-task-assignment-and-path-finding-with-precedence-constraints-17) with [precedence constraints](/publication/2026-08-01-scalable-configuration-search-framework-for-mapf-with-precedence-constraints-15).
- **Risk-aware planning.** [Allocating a shared risk budget across a team](/publication/2026-06-28-risk-bounded-multi-agent-visual-navigation-via-iterative-risk-allocation-12), [risk-bounded reinforcement learning](/publication/2024-05-20-task-driven-risk-bounded-hierarchical-reinforcement-learning-based-on-iterative-refinement) and conditional planning, and [adjusting the tradeoff between efficiency and risk at inference time](/publication/2026-05-01-momo-conditioned-contrastive-representation-learning-for-preference-modulated-planning-16).
- **Learning-guided planning.** Combining learned policies and generative models with search, from [safe visual navigation](/publication/2025-05-19-safe-multi-agent-navigation-guided-by-goal-conditioned-safe-reinforcement-learning-10) and [diffusion-based multi-arm motion planning](/publication/2025-09-27-diffusion-guided-multi-arm-motion-planning-11) to decentralized policies distilled from centralized planners.
